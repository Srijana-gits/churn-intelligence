# Churn Intelligence — final findings

The clearest business opportunity is to investigate short-tenure, month-to-month
customers, especially those using fiber internet. The model helps order a limited
contact list within and beyond that segment. Whether outreach prevents churn
remains unmeasured.

## Evidence

The snapshot contains 7,043 customers, of whom 1,869 churned (26.54%). Churn is
associated most strongly with contract and tenure: month-to-month churn is 42.7%
versus 2.8% on two-year contracts, and tenure of 1–6 months has 53.3% churn versus
9.5% at 49–72 months. These are associations, not intervention effects.

Logistic regression achieves held-out ROC-AUC **0.839** on 1,758 customers, similar
to its training cross-validation result of 0.844. Contacting the top 20% by score
would reach about half the observed churners in that held-out sample. At the
training-OOF-selected threshold, recall is about 74%. Calibration is reasonable
on this dataset, without establishing future-month probabilities.

## Priorities

The retrospective contact list contains 5,163 eligible customers labelled current.
The other 11 current customers have zero tenure and are excluded from scoring;
they remain in descriptive charts. Rank is score × monthly bill, with customer ID
breaking ties. This is a prioritisation measure, not expected losses or savings.

| Bucket | Customers | Share of score × monthly bill |
|---|---:|---:|
| Contact first | 835 | 57.0% |
| Low-cost outreach | 280 | 7.8% |
| Monitor | 1,750 | 24.0% |
| Leave alone | 2,298 | 11.2% |

Buckets use the exact persisted risk threshold, **0.31871380378169745**, and a
monthly bill cutoff of **$64.45**. Earlier results rounded the risk cutoff to
0.32, producing 830 contact-first customers. Using the exact threshold changes
bucket membership but leaves the ranking unchanged.

Risk × bill reaches about 1.3 percentage points more churned billing at a 20%
contact budget than risk alone, while reaching about 2 points fewer churners.
This is a small trade-off. The full-population OOF comparison is exploratory,
because model selection reused the dataset. It is separate from the fixed
held-out model evaluation.

## Recommended action

Use the dashboard to choose a contact capacity and inspect the resulting customer
mix. If preserving the largest number of customers is the goal, compare risk-only
ranking with score × bill. Confirm contact eligibility and available offers using
current operational records before using this historical demonstration.

For a real retention experiment, randomly assign eligible customers within the
chosen cohort to outreach or control. Decide the observation window, primary churn
outcome, offer/contact costs, and required sample size before launching. Measure
incremental retained customers and contribution after costs; churn risk alone
cannot estimate the benefit of contacting a particular customer.

## Limits and next data needs

The data has no dates, prediction-time snapshots, or future outcome windows. A
snapshot classifier cannot substantiate a next-month forecast. Add dated customer
history, contract end dates, support interactions, billing changes, and dated
churn outcomes, then validate on a later time period. The current model misses
most held-out churners on one- or two-year contracts; those additional signals
may help investigate that gap.
